ask: novelty and worth of composition against masked editing for the drain shortcut

deliverable:

verdict: promising but needs rework. As far as a scoped search reached (2026-10-17), nobody has retrained a chest X-ray classifier on drain-removed edits or on generated films and then reported AUROC on real pneumothorax films without a drain. The three-arm comparison in claim 8 is missing two arms that decide it: a model conditioned jointly on both labels, p(x | pneumothorax, no drain), sampled directly; and a cheap baseline that uses no generated films (prevalence recalibration already reaches 0.73 AUROC on the films where the drain points the wrong way). Composition, meaning one model with "contains" labels and sampling p(x)·p(PTX|x)·p(no drain|x), can only beat joint conditioning where the drainless cell is too rare for the joint model to fit. That is the toy's dial, and it can be measured on real films by giving the generators fewer of the real drainless films.

Q1 papers (retraining on edits or generated films, and AUROC on drainless pneumothorax):
- Weng, Pegios, Petersen, Feragen, Bigdeli, ECCV 2024, FastDiME (arXiv 2312.14223). NIH pneumothorax with chest drain as the shortcut. ResNet-18 trained at 100/75/50% drain-pneumothorax association. At 100%: AUROC 0.98 on a test set carrying the same association, 0.65 on a test set without it, 0.65 on drain counterfactuals of that test set; mean absolute prediction change 0.13. This is detection only. The discussion names training on drain-added or drain-removed counterfactuals as an untested next step. Confidence: confident, read in the PDF.
- Pérez-García, Bond-Taylor et al. (Microsoft Health Futures), RadEdit (arXiv 2312.12865 v3). The edit removes the pneumothorax and keeps the drain (edit mask = pneumothorax, keep mask = drain, prompt "No acute cardiopulmonary process", CANDID-PTX), so it builds the drain-without-pneumothorax cell. The edits are used for testing only. Weak classifier: 93.3% accuracy on real biased films, 17.9% on 628 edited films. Strong classifier (SIIM-ACR, with a drain head): 93.7% and 81.7%. No retraining on edits. Confidence: confident, read in the HTML.
- Kina, Petersen 2026, Prevalence calibration as shortcut mitigation (arXiv 2609.07922). Drain shortcut on CheXpert and SIIM-ACR, 75% of positives carrying a drain in training. AUROC on the misaligned films (drainless pneumothorax against drained negatives), CheXpert DenseNet-121, 10 runs: ERM 0.226, ERM recalibrated 0.726, CDAN 0.675, CSM 0.683. SIIM-ACR ERM recalibrated 0.731. No generative baseline. The test set has 100 films in total. Confidence: confident, read in the HTML.
- Cooke et al., RoentMod (arXiv 2509.08640; npj Digital Medicine 2026 per a secondary source, verify). Image-to-image editing adds a pathology. Training with its counterfactuals gives 3 to 19% AUC gains internally and 1 to 11% externally on 5 of 6 pathologies. Its shortcut is off-target pathology, and drains are not mentioned. Confidence: confident on the abstract, body unread.
- sa-spec, Generating Chest X-Ray Counterfactuals by Specialising Foundation Image Models (arXiv 2609.24879, authors not read). Augmentation with counterfactuals that flip the view position, against a view-position-to-effusion shortcut on NIH. The rate at which predictions change under a view intervention falls from 0.593 to 0.115. It uses latent flow matching and does not cover drains. Confidence: likely, from the HTML first half.
- Ktena et al., Nature Medicine 2024, Generative models improve fairness of medical classifiers under distribution shifts. Diffusion-synthetic training data on chest X-ray, dermatology and histopathology. The shifts are demographic and out-of-distribution, and drains are not covered. Confidence: confident on the paper, chest X-ray numbers unread.
- European Radiology 2021, pneumothorax detection with in-image pleural-edge annotations (first author possibly Rueckel, verify). Overall AUROC 0.877 against 0.778 for algorithms trained only on public data, which leaned on tubes. Non-generative. Confidence: likely, from the abstract.
- Answer: none found that retrains on drain-removed edits or generated films and reports AUROC on drainless pneumothorax.

Q2 papers (composition of diffusion models for medical counterfactuals or shortcut mitigation):
- Xia, De Sousa Ribeiro, Rasal, Kori, Mehta, Glocker 2025, Factored Classifier-Free Guidance (arXiv 2506.14399). ε = ε(∅) + Σ_m ω_m (ε(c^m) − ε(∅)). This is Liu et al.'s AND inside one model, which is construction (1). They use it for counterfactuals on MIMIC-CXR (sex, race, pleural effusion) and EMBED mammography (circular skin markers, breast density), with a ResNet-18's AUROC as the measure of intervention effect. No shortcut mitigation and no negation in the half read. Confidence: confident on the formula, second half of the HTML unread.
- Ibrahim et al. 2026, CompDiff (arXiv 2603.16551). Composition inside a learned conditioner (per-attribute tokens plus a composed token) for age×sex×race intersections on MIMIC-CXR and fundus, including intersections held out of training. Classifiers retrained on synthetic films. No score composition and no devices. Confidence: confident, HTML first half.
- Answer: no medical work found that multiplies separately trained experts, uses negation, or uses MCMC samplers. Liu-style AND on one conditional model already exists for medical counterfactuals (FCFG), so construction (1) on chest X-rays is not new as a generator. Its use on a rare cell for shortcut mitigation is new.

What the claim misses:
- Joint conditioning is the generative baseline. NEATX labels drains on 3,709 pneumothorax-positive films, so p(x | PTX, no drain) can be trained and sampled directly. Composition and joint conditioning differ only in how much they borrow from the other cells when the drainless cell is small.
- Memorisation. If the drainless cell holds a few hundred films, samples from either generative arm may sit near those films. Then the gain is no larger than upweighting the real films, which JTT, DFR and recalibration do at no generation cost. Check nearest-neighbour distance to the training films in that cell.
- Editing is not limited to drain removal. RadEdit and RoentMod also add pathology inside a mask, so removing the drain and then growing the pneumothorax region is a two-step edit aimed at the untreated look. "Editing keeps the treated lung" is true of drain-only edits.
- Negation is unbounded with "contains" labels. PTX AND NOT drain targets p(x)·p(PTX|x)·p(drain|x)^(−w), which grows without limit as p(drain|x) → 0, so samples drift toward wherever the drain classifier is most sure there is no drain, off the image manifold if that is easier. The positive label "no drain" gives a bounded factor p(no drain|x) ≤ 1. Use it wherever labels exist.
- Test size. With 25 drainless positives against 25 negatives at AUROC 0.75, the Hanley-McNeil 95% interval is about ±0.14. Kina and Petersen's balanced test has about that many per cell. At 100 against 100 it is about ±0.07. The count of drainless pneumothorax films in NEATX is unknown here and should be counted before the comparison is designed.
- Scoop risk. Weng et al. named this augmentation as their next step, and Petersen is on both the FastDiME and the recalibration paper. That group holds the drain labels, a counterfactual generator and a drain benchmark. A paper reporting only the clinical comparison would likely be overtaken.

Next experiment (the thesis chapter): one classifier, tested on real drainless pneumothorax against drained negatives, in six training arms. (i) ERM. (ii) Prevalence recalibration or DFR. (iii) Plus drain-removed edits. (iv) Plus edits that remove the drain and grow the pneumothorax. (v) Plus samples from the jointly conditioned model. (vi) Plus composed samples (construction 1, "no drain" label, annealed MALA or UHA graded on the toy first). The dial is the share of real drainless films the generators see: 100, 25, 5, 1, 0%. The test films are held out of every arm. The toy predicts that joint conditioning degrades as the share falls, composition holds (0.989 of the product in the rare corner down to a 0.001 share on the four-quadrant toy), and editing stays flat because it never uses those films. Null: composition tracks joint conditioning at every share. Before any classifier is trained, measure pneumothorax size in each arm's films (SIIM-ACR masks or a segmenter) against real drainless films, plus nearest-neighbour distance for memorisation.

What makes the composition line worth a chapter even if editing wins the clinical row: the claim is about the dial, and editing has no dial. The chapter claims that the exact-answer toy predicts, on real films, the rare-cell share below which composition recovers the drainless cell and joint conditioning does not, with samplers graded against exact products first. The clinical comparison is one row of that figure.

changes for the walk: claim 8 is wrong as stated. Repair: "Composition earns its place on the drain shortcut only where drainless pneumothorax is too rare for a jointly conditioned model. That is a dial on the share of real drainless films, measured against editing, joint conditioning and recalibration." The PhD's composition line should claim that the toy's dial curve predicts the real-film dial curve. It should not claim that composition beats editing clinically.
