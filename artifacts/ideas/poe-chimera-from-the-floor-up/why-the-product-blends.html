    What is real here and what is illustrative

    Every dot is a sample drawn in your browser from the distribution named beside it, with a fixed seed so the picture is the same each time. The product samples are drawn from the exact product (the Gaussian product rule), not from a trained model or a sampler. The cat-ness and dog-ness axes in step 4 are an illustration of a feature space, not a measurement of SDXL.
