Where this frame's numbers come from

Shaded numbers are handles: drag one sideways, or focus it and use the arrow keys, and Home resets it. Underlined symbols link to the frame that teaches them.

Every number on the page is computed in the page, from the ten example heights (158, 163, 165, 168, 170, 172, 173, 176, 179, 184 cm, chosen for this series), %%SOURCES%%, except where a note names a file.
