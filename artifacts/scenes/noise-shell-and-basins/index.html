where these numbers come from, and what is drawn
  • The shell. ∥z∥² is a sum of d = 65,536 squared standard normals, a χ²d draw. E∥z∥ = √2·Γ((d+1)/2)/Γ(d/2) ≈ 255.999 and sd(∥z∥) ≈ 1/√2 ≈ 0.707, so the band is 0.707/256 ≈ 0.28% of the radius. Exact math, not a measurement.
  • The empty center. P(∥z∥ < 250) is bounded by the χ² Chernoff bound exp(−(d/2)(t − 1 − ln t)) with t = 250²/d, about 10⁻¹⁶. "Below 10⁻¹⁵" is a safe statement of it.
  • Pipeline settings. 50 DDIM steps and guidance 7.5 are the generation settings used throughout poe_repair_min.
  • Measured in poe_repair_min. A nudge at steps 0 to 10 changed the final image in all 8 tested pairs; the commit point landed between steps 18 and 36. Steps are DDIM indices out of 50.
  • Drawn, not computed. The 2D disc (a slice of 65,536 dimensions), the trajectories, the two modes, the basin arcs, and the boundary positions are illustrative.
  • Explore sampling. 380 seeds per dimension setting; exact χ sampling for d ≤ 1,024, the normal approximation ∥z∥² ≈ d + √(2d)·g above that. The radius is scaled by 1/√d so the shell stays at the same screen radius at every d. Seeds with r > 1.8√d fall off the drawn disc.
