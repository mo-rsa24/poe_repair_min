## The steps

### 1. You: the idea walk

You resume claim 1 of the testbed idea map, with claims 7 and 12 of the when-poe map and the label route's prompt beside it. The walk ends when the testbed's candidate causes of the hybrid are fixed, each with a signature you can compute both in the testbed and from SDXL's cache, and when the parser that scores outputs in drawing parameters has its categories.

**Prompt:** [the idea walk](prompts/11-1-the-testbed-idea-walk.md)

⏱ you ~1 to 2 h over two sittings (estimate: it resumes with three files beside it, and the median measured `drip-idea` session is 20 minutes) · AI ~30 min · stop: the candidate causes and the parser are fixed

### 2. You: build the testbed

Once that walk compiles, build its design with `/drip-execute-plan` on the plan its compile writes. That plan's pieces set the build's time.

The parser and the timing come first: the parser is trusted only above 99% correct on 1,000 held-out training draws and on 200 hand-built hybrids, and the smallest model has to reach 95% correct single-concept samples in under 30 minutes.

Before build A, open the [text part of the SDXL notebook scene](https://claude.ai/artifact/1GayZthQkRL4FmukdCV9WW#nb-text): "a cat and a dog" is padded to 77 tokens and run through SDXL's two encoders, the machinery build A shrinks to one learned embedding per label. Before build B, open its [attention part](https://claude.ai/artifact/1GayZthQkRL4FmukdCV9WW#nb-attn): image tokens query the text tokens through cross-attention, which is the route build B gives your embeddings and the place leakage and dropped concepts can start.

**Prompt:** [build the testbed](prompts/11-2-build-the-testbed.md)

⏱ set by the plan the walk compiles
